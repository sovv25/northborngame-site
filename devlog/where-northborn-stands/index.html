Devlog 1 24 November 2026

Where Northborn stands

The site is up, so this is a good moment to write down what already works in Northborn, and what does not yet.

Northborn: The First Viking is a survival RPG set in Norway in 792. You are a younger son with a leaking roof and your father's debt, and the world around you keeps going whether you do well or not. Most of the work so far has gone into that world, not into how it looks.

A world on its own clock

Everything in Northborn runs on game time. There is a calendar with seasons, days that get light and dark, and weather that changes with the time of year. Nothing grows or rots "per frame": a crop knows when it was sown, and works out how far it has come when you look at it. That is what lets the world keep living while you are somewhere else.

Staying alive

You get hungry, thirsty and cold. Wind makes the cold worse. If you ignore it for long enough it starts to hurt you. You can eat and drink, and you have to sleep. All the numbers are placeholders for now; the point was to get the pieces talking to each other first.

Food from the land

  • Farming. Fields you till, sow and harvest, with crops that grow by the season.
  • Fishing. Make a rod, cast a line, and bring home perch, trout or salmon for a fish stew.
  • Snares. Set them, walk away, and come back to see if something was caught.
  • Butchering. An animal you kill or catch becomes meat, hide and bone, and it will rot if you leave it.

A pack, a workbench, a body

The old debug panels are being replaced with real screens. There is now a Pack sheet with what you carry and what you wear, and beside it whatever bag or chest you have open, so you can move things straight between them. There is a Crafting sheet, and a Body sheet that shows how you are doing.

The man himself

The player character got a lean body, linen trousers and the tied-back hair from the concept art. He can stand, walk, swing an axe and aim a bow while walking in any direction. You can see him, in his clothes, on the Pack sheet.

What comes next

  • Putting the turf house pieces into the world, so the farm looks like a farm.
  • More hairstyles, faces and beards for the character.
  • A first proper look at the coast, the light and the grass in play.

There are no in-game screenshots on this site yet. The paintings are concept art. Real footage goes up on YouTube as it gets ready.
