Before there were Vikings, there were farmers.

Northborn is a survival RPG set in a Norse world that is already alive when you arrive. You do not start as a hero. You start as a younger son with a leaking roof, and you earn everything after that.

I

Your father's farm

Your father is in his mound. What he left you is a turf house whose roof lets the rain in, a field gone to weeds, and your little sister, who works as hard as you do.

He also left you what he owed. It falls due at Winter Nights, when the year turns cold.

II

A world that does not wait

Down the fjord there are other farms, a market town, and people with troubles of their own. They fish, they trade, they argue at the Thing. Their year goes on whether you do well or not.

Here every piece of silver is earned, and every one of them counts.

Three seasons to pay what you owe

One year on the farm

  1. Spring

    Mend the roof. Plough the field. Sow.

  2. Summer

    Fish, hunt and trade. Hear the news at the Thing.

  3. Autumn

    Bring in the harvest. Pay what your father owed.

  4. Winter

    Live on what you put away. Keep the fire going.

III

Word from the west

“West, across the sea, there are houses of stone full of silver. The men who keep them carry no swords.”

A trader from far away, at the market

No one from the fjord has ever sailed there.

IV

Be the first

Build a longship. Find men brave enough, or poor enough, to row it. Sail where no one from the north has gone to take what they want.

Come home with silver and a name. Buy land, take in the people who come to a farm that has a name, and grow it into a village before the next winter.

What it is built on

Six rules

  1. I

    The world lives without you

    People, trade and the year go on. You start inside it, not alone in it.

  2. II

    Survival comes first

    Cold, hunger, wounds and the seasons. Winter is what you spend the year getting ready for.

  3. III

    Every action takes time

    Felling a tree, cooking a meal, raising a wall. Nothing is instant, and the light goes while you work.

  4. IV

    Everything is connected

    The season decides the harvest, the harvest decides who you can feed, the weather decides who stays warm.

  5. V

    Growth has a reason

    Every building answers a need you have felt. A shelter before a house, a house before a hall.

  6. VI

    Raids have weight

    Everyone you take west is someone who is not home for the harvest.
